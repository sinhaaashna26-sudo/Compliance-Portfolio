# Compliance Audit Report – Ashna Sinha

## 📑 Executive Summary
This report summarizes the compliance audit conducted on core financial processes.  
**Scope**: KYC verification, transaction monitoring, and account closure SOPs.  
**Excluded**: HR policies, IT systems, and non-financial operational areas.  
**Objective**: Ensure adherence to RBI/SEBI guidelines and internal SOPs.  

---

## 🔍 Findings
1. **KYC Verification**  
   - Strength: Proper documentation collected.  
   - Gap: Inconsistent address proof validation.  

2. **Transaction Monitoring**  
   - Strength: Alerts generated for large cash deposits.  
   - Gap: Offshore transfers not flagged consistently.  

3. **Account Closure**  
   - Strength: SOP followed for inactive accounts.  
   - Gap: Delay in closure approvals.  

---

## 🛠️ Recommendations
- Standardize address proof validation across all branches.  
- Enhance monitoring rules for tax haven transfers.  
- Automate account closure approvals to reduce delays.  

---

## 📊 Compliance Metrics
- **Error Rate**: 5% (target <2%)  
- **Testing Volume**: 100 accounts reviewed  
- **Productivity**: 20 accounts per auditor per day  

---

## 📝 Reporting Framework
Reporting is the stage where audit results are converted into insights. A strong report includes:  

1. **Executive Summary** → Short overview of scope and results.  
   - Example: *“Audit conducted on KYC verification and transaction monitoring. Overall compliance level: 85%.”*  

2. **Findings** → Strengths and gaps discovered.  
   - Example: *“Address proof validation inconsistent across branches.”*  

3. **Recommendations** → Practical fixes.  
   - Example: *“Standardize address proof validation and automate account closure approvals.”*  

4. **Compliance Metrics** → Numbers that show performance.  
   - Example: *“Error rate: 5% (target <2%).”*  

---

## 🎯 Why It Matters
- Shows recruiters you can **evaluate compliance processes**, not just write SOPs.  
- Demonstrates **analytical thinking and structured reporting**.  
- Prepares you for interview questions like:  
  *“How would you conduct a compliance audit?”*  

**Example Interview Answer:**  
*“I would start by defining the scope — for example, KYC verification. Then I’d test a sample of accounts, record findings such as missing documents, and calculate metrics like error rate. Finally, I’d prepare a report with recommendations, such as standardizing document checks or automating approvals.”*
