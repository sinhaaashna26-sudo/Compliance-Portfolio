# KYC & AML Cheat Sheet – Ashna Sinha

## 🔑 KYC (Know Your Customer)
- **Purpose**: Verify customer identity and prevent fraud.
- **Documents**: Passport, Aadhaar, PAN, Utility bill, Bank statement.
- **Process**: Collect → Validate → Match → Approve/Reject.

## 🛡️ AML (Anti-Money Laundering)
- **Goal**: Prevent illegal money movement.
- **Suspicious Activity**: Large cash deposits, frequent transfers.
- **Transaction Monitoring**: Continuous tracking of customer activity.
- **Red Flags**: Structuring deposits, offshore accounts, sudden high-value transfers, tax haven transfers.

## 🌍 Key Term – Tax Haven
- **Definition**: A country or territory offering very low or zero taxes and strong financial secrecy laws.  
- **Examples**: Cayman Islands, Bermuda, Luxembourg, Panama.  
- **Risk**: Often used to hide money, avoid taxes, or launder funds.  
- **Compliance Note**: Sudden transfers to tax havens without clear business purpose are flagged as suspicious.

## 📘 Practice Scenarios
1. **Structuring**: Customer deposits ₹9,90,000 three times in a week to avoid ₹10 lakh reporting threshold.  
2. **Offshore Transfers**: Large unexplained wire transfers to tax-haven accounts.  
3. **Luxury Purchase**: Sudden high-value cash purchase inconsistent with income.  
4. **Layering**: Frequent small transfers abroad to disguise money origin.
